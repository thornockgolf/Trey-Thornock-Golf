About Trey

Meet Trey

Bio

Trey Thornock is a PGA golf coach focused on helping golfers of all ages and skill levels improve through personalized instruction and purposeful practice. Based primarily at Dick’s House of Sport in Durham, Trey uses technology such as TrackMan, video analysis, and Vertex putting sensors to help players better understand their game and track their progress. He works with beginners, juniors, recreational golfers, and competitive players through individualized lessons and player-development programs. Trey also teaches periodically in Douglas, Wyoming, helping golfers take what they learn from practice to the course.

PGA Credentials

  • PGA Member
  • TrackMan University Level 2
  • Mizuno and Callaway product knowledge & club fitting certification
  • Master Club Fitter
  • Club Repair Technician
  • ADM Certified
  • PGA HOPE Certified

Coaching Focus

Trey’s coaching skills include short game, putting, tournament strategy, course strategy, wedge play, full swing, wind play, and club fitting — applied through an assessment-driven approach built around Trey’s player-development pathway.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you teach beginners?

Yes. Trey works with complete beginners and adults learning the game for the first time.

Do you teach junior golfers?

Yes. Junior coaching is a major part of Trey’s business, including the U.S. Kids Golf program.

Do you work with competitive high school golfers?

Yes. Trey has coached a high school golfer to a State Championship and works with competitive junior golfers regularly.

Do you coach PGA professionals?

Yes. Trey has coached PGA professionals to improve their games, in addition to golfers of every other skill level.

What happens during the first lesson?

The process starts with a conversation about your goals and where your game is today, followed by an assessment of your current performance before deciding what to focus on.

What is a Golf Performance Assessment?

An evaluation of the golfer as a whole — ball flight, TrackMan data, contact, and player goals — used to identify the biggest opportunities for improvement and recommend a coaching path.

How does TrackMan fit into a lesson?

TrackMan provides objective data on things like club speed, launch, spin, and strike patterns. Trey uses that data to help you understand cause and effect, not just to hand you a list of numbers.
