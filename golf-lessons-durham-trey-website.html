Golf Instruction, Done Right

PGA Golf Coaching Built Around the Individual Golfer

Every golfer who walks into a lesson is different — different swing, different goals, different starting point. Trey Thornock Golf uses an assessment-driven coaching system (Assess, Identify, Develop, Practice, Transfer, Perform) instead of a one-size-fits-all swing method, so every lesson connects to the one before it and builds toward a clear next step. That’s the difference between collecting swing tips and actually playing better golf.

What to Expect

What Happens During a Golf Lesson

A golf lesson with Trey follows the same clear process every time, so you always know what you’re working on and why.

  1. ConnectTrey starts by understanding your goals and where your game is today.
  2. AssessYour current performance is observed — using TrackMan data and video where helpful — before deciding what needs attention.
  3. FocusTogether you identify the one priority that will make the biggest difference, instead of changing everything at once.
  4. TrainSimple instruction and purposeful practice, built around that priority.
  5. TransferYou take the new skill from the lesson bay to the golf course.
  6. PlanYou leave knowing exactly what to work on before your next lesson.
Coaching Approach

Technology & Data With a Purpose

Trey coaches with TrackMan’s newest 3D motion capture technology, combining ball flight data with full-body swing motion. TrackMan provides the data — coaching determines what to do with it. As a PGA Member with TrackMan University Level 2 certification, Trey uses that data to help golfers understand cause and effect, not just hand them a list of numbers.
